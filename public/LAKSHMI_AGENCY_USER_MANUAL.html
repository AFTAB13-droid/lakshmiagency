The Complete 9-Step Agency Operational Workflow

Lakshmi Agency operates on a disciplined, automated, and fraud-resistant 9-step campaign lifecycle. Every deal follows this exact lifecycle:

1

Admin Deal Creation & Slot Allocation

The Master Admin creates a deal in Deals Studio (/admin/deals). Specific quotas are assigned for Rating Slots vs. Review Slots, pricing model (Full Refund, Less Deduction, or Commission Bonus), and marketplace link.

2

Mediator Campaign Sharing

Mediators access their Live Deals Desk (/mediator/deals) and use the 1-Click WhatsApp Pitch Generator. The system automatically injects their unique Mediator Code (e.g. #LAX096) and pre-fills the Google Order Form URL.

3

Buyer Purchase on Marketplace

The Buyer views the deal on the portal (/buyer/deals) and clicks the branded "Buy on Amazon →" or "Buy on Flipkart →" button. The purchase is placed natively on the marketplace at standard consumer price.

4

Google Order Form Submission (Step 2 in Portal)

Immediately after purchasing, the buyer completes the Google Order Form with the Marketplace Order ID, product details, screenshot, and Mediator Code. The webhook engine instantly syncs this to Lakshmi Agency and decrements the remaining deal slot.

5

Holding Period (4–5 Days Delivery Window)

The order enters the mandatory holding window. The buyer waits for the product to arrive, uses it, and posts an authentic review on the marketplace once 4 to 5 days have elapsed.

6

Live Review Verification

The marketplace approves and publishes the buyer's product review. The buyer takes a clear screenshot displaying the 5-star rating, review headline, and text.

7

Google Refund Form Submission (20-Day Rule)

The buyer opens the Google Refund Form (must be completed within 20 days of delivery). They submit the live review screenshot, their UPI ID / Bank details, and the Marketplace Order ID. The portal updates the order status to REFUND FORM SUBMITTED.

8

Agency Dual-Verification & Security Audit

The Admin team accesses the Master Orders Hub (/admin/orders), reviews the Order Form screenshot against the live review screenshot, verifies buyer identity, and confirms deal rules.

9

Reimbursement Disbursement (20–25 Working Days)

The approved refund amount is disbursed directly to the buyer's UPI or bank account within 20 to 25 working days. The admin logs the official Bank UTR or UPI transaction reference into the system.

Google Forms & Sheets Webhook Setup Guide

Lakshmi Agency syncs in real-time with Google Sheets via a Google Apps Script webhook trigger. Follow this 2-minute setup guide inside the sheet linked to your Google Form(s):

⚡ 4-Step Google Sheet Setup Instructions
  1. Open the Google Sheet linked to your Google Form.
  2. Click top menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any default code and paste the code from src/scripts/googleAppsScript.js.
  4. Click the Triggers (⏰) icon on the left menu → Click + Add Trigger:
    • Function: onFormSubmit
    • Event Source: From spreadsheet
    • Event Type: On form submit
    • Click Save and accept Google permissions.
🔧 Troubleshooting: How to Re-sync Failed Submissions

If any row in your Google Sheet shows ⚠️ SYNC PENDING:

  1. Open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. In the function dropdown at the top, select syncAllPendingRows.
  3. Click Run (▶).
  4. The script will immediately push all pending rows to https://www.lakshmiagency.net and update the sheet to ✅ ORDER REGISTERED & SLOTS DEDUCTED!

Official Agency Rules & Compliance Policies

Rule #1 (Order Form Mandate): The Google Order Form must be completed immediately after placing the order on Amazon/Flipkart. Orders not registered via the Order Form cannot be claimed or refunded.
Rule #2 (Review Holding Window): Product reviews must only be submitted after 4 to 5 days of delivery. Immediate or premature reviews trigger marketplace fraud filters and will be rejected.
Rule #3 (20-Day Refund Claim Deadline): The Google Refund Form must be completed within 20 calendar days of delivery. Claims submitted after 20 days are permanently closed.
Rule #4 (Reimbursement Timeline): Verified refunds are disbursed within 20 to 25 working days (excluding weekends and government holidays).
Rule #5 (Non-Refundable Charges): Cash-on-Delivery (COD) charges, marketplace shipping fees, and convenience charges are non-refundable. Only the agreed product amount is refunded.
Rule #6 (Anti-Fraud Cross-Verification): Each Marketplace Order ID is unique. Duplicate submissions, edited review screenshots, or cancelled orders will be blacklisted across all agency campaigns.
